I've lived in two different apartments in Chicago. My first was in a mid-rise towards the west side of town in between Wicker Park and Bucktown. A year after living there, I moved to a high-rise in River North on the 25th floor.

In River North, our apartment has a south-facing balcony where we can often see the sunset to the west casting beautiful colors onto the skyscrapers in the city. I've taken quite a few pictures from there.
